## NEW VLA-SPECIFIC VENUES (2025-2026)

The VLA field exploded in 2025-2026. Here are new venues that specifically target VLA models:

1. **ICRA 2026 WS: VLA Pipelines for Real Robots** (NEW 2026) - First dedicated VLA deployment workshop. Includes competition track with 10K+ hours of robot data. Organized by AIRoA.

2. **CVPR 2026 WS: 2nd 3D-LLM/VLA Workshop** (started CVPR 2025) - Dedicated to 3D perception + VLA. Growing rapidly.

3. **CVPR 2026 WS: CV in the Wild** (pivoted to VLA focus in 2026) - 5th edition pivoted specifically to LMM + VLA systems this year.

4. **ICLR 2026 had 164 VLA submissions** - Not a separate venue, but signals VLA is now a first-class topic at top ML venues.

5. **CoRL 2026 WS: SAFE-ROL** (2nd edition) and **Resource-Rational Robot Learning** - Both directly relevant to edge VLA deployment and safety.

6. **WCCI 2026 WS: Generative Foundation Models for Robotics** - New workshop at IEEE WCCI focusing on generative models including VLAs.

**Trend:** No standalone VLA conference exists yet, but dedicated workshops are proliferating at every major robotics and vision venue. CoRL and ICRA are the natural homes. Expect a standalone workshop series or symposium by 2027.

## RECOMMENDED SUBMISSION STRATEGY FOR VLA-EDGE

### Immediate (Apr-May 2026)
1. **ICRA WS: VLA Pipelines** (Apr 15) - 2-4 page abstract on edge deployment profiling + safety. Non-archival, low risk.
2. **CVPR WS: 3D-LLM/VLA** (Apr 26) - 2-8 page paper on VLA inference optimization.
3. **NeurIPS 2026** (May 6) - Full paper on action-centric quantization or safety verification method.
4. **CVPR WS: CV in the Wild** (May 16) - 8-page paper on VLA deployment in real-world conditions.
5. **CoRL 2026** (May 29) - Full paper on vla-edge system: safety contracts + edge deployment + profiling.

### Medium-term (Jun-Sep 2026)
6. **RTSS 2026** (May 21) - Real-time VLA inference analysis.
7. **RA-L** (submit ~Jul 2026) - Systems paper with ICRA 2027 presentation option.
8. **CoRL WS: SAFE-ROL / Resource-Rational** (~Sep 2026) - Workshop papers on safety/efficiency.

### Long-term (Oct 2026 - Mar 2027)
9. **ICRA 2027** (~Sep 2026) - Full systems paper with Jetson benchmarks.
10. **ICLR 2027** (~Oct 2026) - Method paper on quantization or safety.
11. **CVPR 2027** (~Nov 2026) - Vision encoder optimization for VLAs.
12. **GTC 2027** (~Dec 2026) - Demo/talk proposal for Jetson deployment.

### Paper archetypes that fit vla-edge:
- **Systems paper** (RA-L, ICRA, CoRL): Full toolkit - profiling, safety contracts, edge deployment
- **Method paper** (NeurIPS, ICLR): Action-centric quantization, safety verification method
- **Benchmark paper** (NeurIPS Datasets track, CoRL): VLA edge deployment benchmark
- **Demo/short paper** (ICRA WS, CVPR WS): Specific result - e.g., 10 Hz VLA on Jetson Orin
- **Analysis paper** (RSS, RTSS): VLA inference characterization, real-time analysis
